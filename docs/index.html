Start here

New to WoW? Read this first

Everything you need for your first evenings. You can skip the rest of the page until you're around level 20.

1 · The basics
  • Move with WASD. Hold the right mouse button to steer the camera. Space jumps.
  • A yellow ! over someone's head means a quest to take. A yellow ? means a quest to hand in. Right-click them.
  • L opens your quest log, M the map, B your bags, C your character, P your spells, N your talents.
  • Tab targets the nearest enemy. Right-click an enemy to start attacking, and right-click its body after it dies to loot it.
  • If you die: click "Release Spirit", run back to your body as a ghost, and click "Resurrect". It costs nothing but time.
  • Hearthstone: talk to the innkeeper in Brill and pick "Make this inn your home". Use the stone in your bags to teleport back there, once every 30 minutes.
  • New bags don't equip themselves. When you get one, open your bags and drag it onto an empty bag slot next to the backpack at the bottom right.
2 · Your buttons

New spells don't appear on your bar by themselves. Open your spellbook with P and drag each one onto your bar in this order, so your fingers learn one layout. A spell shows up in the spellbook once you've trained it.

  • 1 Seal of Righteousness: always keep it switched on.
  • 2 Judgement (level 4): your opening hit. Press it again whenever it lights up.
  • 3 Holy Strike (level 6): press it whenever it lights up.
  • 4 Consecration (level 20): for 2 or more enemies at once.
  • 5 Hammer of Justice (level 8): stuns an enemy, which is great for a spare enemy.
  • 6 Holy Light: heals yourself. It's slow, so cast it when nothing is hitting you.
  • 7 Divine Protection (level 6): "oh no" button. It makes you hard to hurt so you can heal.
  • 8 Lay on Hands (level 10): "nearly dead" button. It heals you to full, about once an hour.

Set and forget: Blessing of Might on yourself (it lasts an hour) and Devotion Aura, or Retribution Aura from 16.

3 · A fight, step by step
  1. Before you start, check that the Seal (1) and Blessing of Might are on. Their icons show at the top right of your screen.
  2. Target one enemy with Tab, then press 2 Judgement to start the fight.
  3. Right-click it so you auto-attack. Press 3 whenever it lights up, and 2 again when it's back.
  4. If a second enemy joins: press 5 to stun one, and from level 20 also 4.
  5. If your health bar (green, top left) drops below a third mid-fight: 7, then 6. If you're nearly dead: 8.
  6. After the fight: heal with 6, or use Cannibalize on a human or undead body. Then pick the next enemy.
4 · Every time you level up
  • Even levels (2, 4, 6…): visit the paladin trainer (Shari Stilwell in Brill, or the Undercity) and buy everything. Then press P and drag any brand-new spell onto your bar; the game won't do it for you.
  • From level 10: you get a talent point every level. Press N, open the Retribution tab, and follow the list in "Playing Retribution" further down.
  • Quest rewards: when you can pick one, take the one with Strength or the bigger two-handed weapon.
  • Tick your new level on the slider at the top of this page, and the page shows where to go next.
5 · Gear: what's better?
  • The stats you want, in order: Strength first, then Stamina (more health), then Intellect (more mana).
  • Weapon: a two-handed sword, axe or mace. Hover over it and compare the "damage per second" number; bigger is better.
  • Armor: wear mail until 40, then plate.
  • Grey items are junk, so sell them to any vendor. Green and better ones: equip them if they're better, sell them on the Auction House otherwise.
  • Install the Pawn addon: it shows a green arrow on items that are upgrades for you.
6 · Rules that keep you alive
  • One enemy at a time. Fight at the edge of a group, not in the middle.
  • Look at the enemy's level next to its portrait. Red, or a skull, means too strong, so walk away.
  • A gold dragon around the portrait means an "elite". Only fight those with your friend.
  • Start each fight with full health and mana. Sit down and drink if you need to; patience beats running back as a ghost.
  • Running away rarely works. If a fight goes badly, use 7 and 8 and finish it.
  • Leave Righteous Fury off. You can train it at level 10, but it makes every enemy attack you, which is a tank's job. If you see its buff icon, right-click the icon to cancel it.
7 · Playing with your friend
  • To group up, right-click their portrait or name and choose "Invite". In a group you both get credit for kills and quest items.
  • To share a quest, select it in your quest log (L) and click "Share Quest".
  • To follow them, right-click their portrait and choose "Follow". This is handy for long runs.
  • Let the warlock's big blue demon (the Voidwalker) hit first. Wait two seconds, then join in, so enemies stay on the demon instead of you.
  • Buff each other. Give the warlock Blessing of Wisdom. They'll give you a Healthstone (use it when low) and a Soulstone (it lets you come back to life on the spot).
8 · Words people use
Mob
Any enemy creature.
Pull
Starting a fight, or the group of enemies you fight.
Aggro
Who the enemy is attacking. "Pulled aggro" = it's attacking you now.
Add
An extra enemy that joins a fight.
Tank / Heal / DPS
The three group jobs: take hits, heal, deal damage. You're DPS.
Buff / Debuff
A good effect on you, or a bad effect on an enemy.
CD
Cooldown: the wait before a spell works again.
OOM
Out of mana.
AoE
Hits everything in an area, like Consecration.
Ding
"I just levelled up."
Rez
Bring a dead player back to life.
Wipe
The whole group died.
Instance / Dungeon
A private area for your group, with harder enemies and better loot.

Chapter I

Your kit

What makes an Undead Warlock in Forever different from the one you may remember.

Forsaken paladins are new in Forever, and the paladin class itself was reworked.

Undead racials

Will of the Forsaken

Breaks Charm, Fear and Sleep (2 min cooldown). In Forever it leaves no 5-second immunity window, so you can be feared again right away. Hold it for the second fear in a pull, not the first.

Touch of the Grave

Passive. 30% chance on hit to drain up to 5% of your max health (15 s internal cooldown).

Cannibalize

Restores 7% health and mana every 2 s for 10 s from a Humanoid or Undead corpse within 5 yards. Use it after every pull of humanoids to skip eating.

Underwater Breathing: breath lasts 300% longer.

Paladin changes in Forever
  • Judgement no longer uses up your Seal. Judge on cooldown and keep the Seal running.
  • Holy Strike (level 6): an instant Holy-damage weapon strike with a 10–12 s cooldown (sources differ). Use it between auto-attacks.
  • Seal of Fury (level 10): the new tanking Seal. With a shield it grants absorbs, and judging it taunts.
  • Righteous Fury moved to level 10 (was 16): +33% threat, +90% on Holy damage. It's for tanking only, so keep it off as Retribution.
  • Consecration and Blessing of Kings are baseline at 20, with no talents needed.
  • Blessings last 1 hour. Blessing of Salvation and Blessing of Light are gone.
  • Unsealed Wrath is the 31-point Retribution capstone and brings back Seal twisting: hits store up to 3 copies of your Seal for 15 s, and a hit with a different Seal procs them all.
  • Two-Handed Weapon Specialization now boosts Holy and Physical damage and cuts your threat by up to 20% with a two-hander, so the Voidwalker keeps aggro more easily.
  • Talent points: 21 at 30 and 51 at 60, and each tree gains an extra milestone at 16 points.
Seals, blessings, auras
  • Seal of Righteousness: use it with fast weapons and early on.
  • Seal of Command: a Retribution talent here, not baseline. Best with a slow two-hander like Wolfsbane.
  • Blessing of Might on yourself. Give the warlock Blessing of Wisdom, and from 20, Blessing of Kings.
  • Retribution Aura (16) while questing, Devotion Aura when things get hairy.
  • Exorcism (20) and Holy Wrath (50) hit the undead and demons hard. Use them in the Plaguelands and Scholomance.
  • Lay on Hands (10) and Divine Shield (34) are your emergency buttons.
Warlock changes in Forever
  • Demons scale with your stats. This is the best change for Demonology: gear upgrades for you also upgrade your demon. Stamina counts less than it did in beta, though: each point gives your demon 2 health (was 7.5).
  • Felguard and Metamorphosis are not in the game. Demonology's capstone is Demonic Pact.
  • DoTs and drains can crit.
  • Life Tap scales with Spirit, not spell damage.
  • Banes: Agony and Doom are Banes. You can keep one Bane and one Curse on the same target.
  • Curse of the Elements now raises damage taken from every magic school.
  • Firestone and Spellstone are weapon imbues that stack with weapon oils. Spellstone boosts only Shadow damage, which suits Shadow Bolt.
  • Soul Shards: a Drain Soul killing blow always grants one. Shards from Drain Soul damage ticks were cut sharply at launch, so don't count on them.
Which demon, when
  • Imp (1–10): ranged fire damage and the Blood Pact stamina buff.
  • Voidwalker (10+): your main questing demon. Demonology talents make it a real tank.
  • Succubus or Incubus (20+, both offered in Forever): kills single mobs faster when you can take a few hits. Seduction is crowd control in dungeons.
  • Felhunter (30+): Devour Magic and Spell Lock against casters.
  • Demonic Pact (capstone): sacrifice one demon for its buff, then summon another and keep both.

    Playing Demonology

    Your demon tanks and you deal damage from behind it. Demonology is slower to kill than Affliction, but you will rarely die. At 40, dual spec lets you add a second build without giving this one up.

    Chapter IV · advanced

    Playing Retribution

    Use a big two-hander and a Seal, judge on cooldown, and heal yourself between pulls. Current guides rate Retribution as the easiest paladin leveling spec. At 40, dual spec adds Protection for dungeons. New player? The buttons and the step-by-step fight in Start here are all you need. Use this section only for the talent list.

    Talent priority

    Press N, open the Retribution tab, and spend each new point going down this list. You get one point per level from 10.

    10–20
    Seal of Command (a Retribution talent in Forever), then Conviction (+6% crit on everything) and Sanctified Judgement.
    20–30
    Unyielding Faith, Two-Handed Weapon Specialization (more damage, less threat) and Vengeance (needs Seal of Command, stacks 5 times).
    30–39
    Champion of the Light (spell damage from Intellect, faster Hammer of Wrath), and Repentance, which the capstone requires.
    40
    Unsealed Wrath, the 31-point capstone, for Seal twisting.
    Skip
    Vindication no longer gives Attack Power. Take Turn Evil only if you run Scarlet and Scholomance a lot.

    verify Updated from Blizzard's Nov 2 notes (Twist of Light and Instrument of Law were removed). The order of talents those notes don't mention is still from beta, so check a Forever talent calculator.

    Solo pull
    1. Seal up: Seal of Command with a slow two-hander, Seal of Righteousness otherwise.
    2. Open with Judgement. Your Seal stays up.
    3. Holy Strike on cooldown, between swings.
    4. On 2+ mobs, from 20: drop Consecration under them.
    5. For burst, Hammer of Justice, then judge on the stunned target.
    6. Between pulls: Cannibalize on humanoid or undead corpses, then Flash of Light if you need more.
    Trouble & dungeons
    • On a bad pull: Divine Protection (6), then Divine Shield (34) and a Hearthstone or a heal. Lay on Hands when you're nearly dead.
    • Stop runners with Seal of Justice (22) or Hammer of Wrath (44).
    • Put Blessing of Protection on a party member who pulls aggro. Blessing of Freedom breaks roots and slows.
    • In dungeons, use Seal of Fury to taunt if the tank dies, Turn Undead (24) in Scarlet and Scholomance, and Redemption after a wipe.
    • Blessings: Might on melee, Wisdom on casters and healers, Kings on everyone.
    Talent priority
    10–20
    Demonic Embrace and Improved Voidwalker: more Stamina for you and a sturdier Voidwalker. Your Stamina now adds little to the demon's health, so Improved Voidwalker matters more.
    20–30
    Fel Domination: an instant demon summon when your demon dies, which saves pulls that go wrong. Then Fel Stamina and Fel Intellect.
    30–40
    Unholy Power and Master Summoner, then Master Demonologist (bonus effect based on which demon is out).
    40+
    Soul Link, if Forever's tree still has it (it shares damage with your demon). Then Demonic Sacrifice, then the Demonic Pact capstone.
    Spare points
    Improved Corruption in Affliction, for faster kills.

    verify These are the Classic talent names, and Forever rebalanced every tree. Check the Zockify talent calculator before spending points; the goal is the same either way: survival first, then demon damage.

    Solo pull
    1. Send the Voidwalker in. Wait for one Torment or a couple of hits so it has threat.
    2. Cast Corruption, then Bane of Agony, then a Curse.
    3. Shadow Bolt or wand until the mob is low.
    4. If the Voidwalker drops low, use Health Funnel. Don't let it die mid-pull.
    5. Finish with Drain Soul for a guaranteed shard.
    6. Between pulls: Life Tap, then Cannibalize.
    Trouble & dungeons
    • On an extra mob: Fear it, or let the Voidwalker taunt it with Suffering.
    • If the demon dies, use Fel Domination plus an instant summon.
    • Sacrifice the Voidwalker for a shield, then Healthstone.
    • Keep a Soulstone on yourself before risky pulls.
    • In dungeons, run the Imp (Blood Pact), or the Succubus if the tank needs crowd control. Apply Curse of the Elements, hand out Healthstones, and use Ritual of Summoning to bring players in.
    Playing as a pair · Warlock + Paladin
    • Pulls: the Voidwalker opens, the paladin drops Consecration on the pack, and the warlock spreads Corruption and Banes. Let the Voidwalker hold threat for a couple of seconds first.
    • Buffs: Blessing of Wisdom or Kings on the warlock, Might on the paladin. The Imp's Blood Pact raises both of your Stamina early on.
    • Safety: the warlock Soulstones the paladin, the paladin Redemptions the warlock, and Healthstones go to both. Blessing of Protection saves the warlock if something breaks through.
    • Downtime: you both have Cannibalize, so split the corpses. The warlock Life Taps while the paladin heals them back up.
    • Professions: Tailoring and Enchanting on the warlock, Mining and Blacksmithing on the paladin. The enchanter enchants the paladin's two-hander. Swap materials instead of selling them.
    • Dungeons: run the Wolfsbane dungeons together. After 40, a Protection paladin and a Demonology warlock can build a group around themselves.

    Chapter VII

    Professions, gold & addons

    Professions

    Mining and Blacksmithing fits a two-hander paladin: Blacksmithing makes your weapons and plate, and Mining supplies the ore. It also complements the warlock's Tailoring and Enchanting.

    For more gold, run Mining and Herbalism to 40, then swap Herbalism for Blacksmithing. Minor Healing Potions are now a First Aid recipe.

    Gold plan
    • The level 40 Warhorse and dual spec (10g) come due at the same time, so start saving in your 30s.
    • Sell ore and bars on the Auction House; they sell steadily all through leveling.
    • Training every spell from 1 to 60 costs about 206g in total, the same for every class. Skip the ranks of spells you don't use, like the resistance auras.
    • Cannibalize and self-heals mean you rarely buy food.
    Professions

    Tailoring and Enchanting is the classic warlock pairing. Tailoring turns the cloth you loot into your own gear and bags. Enchanting turns the gear you don't need into enchanting materials, and early on it makes wands, which are a big upgrade at levels 10–20.

    For more gold early, take Herbalism and Skinning or Mining to 40, then switch. Minor Healing Potions are now a First Aid recipe. Leveling crafting professions also earns Legacy Points.

    Gold plan
    • Forever has no WoW Token and no paid boosts, so all gold comes from playing.
    • Savings targets: 10g for dual spec at 40, plus training at every even level. Training from 1 to 60 costs about 206g in total. Warlock training is cheaper by what the demon Grimoires cost, so you still need gold for those.
    • Sell cloth stacks and leather on the Auction House. Disenchant the green items you don't need.
    • Skip vendor food: Cannibalize and Life Tap cover most of your downtime.
    • Look into the new systems: Waylaid Crates, Merchant's Favor and the Azeroth Commerce Authority recipes.
    Addons

    Install the Forever file of each addon: Classic Era and Retail files won't load. Launch-day status comes from a pre-launch guide, so verify each one.

    • Grimoire Sync: the addon that feeds this page.
    • Questie: quest givers and objectives on the map. Use the official project only. The "Questie Forever" upload isn't from the Questie team. Expect gaps for brand-new quests at first.
    • RestedXP: step-by-step route, free to about level 22 for Horde.
    • Leatrix Plus + Leatrix Maps: quality-of-life toggles and better maps.
    • ThreatClassic2: threat meter. Useful when the Voidwalker tanks and in dungeons.
    • Details! and Deadly Boss Mods or BigWigs (pick one): meters and boss timers.
    • AtlasLoot Classic Forever, Talents Forever, What's Training?: loot tables, the reworked talents, trainer costs.
    • Auctionator: auction house. TradeSkillMaster and official WeakAuras weren't ready for launch.

    Safe installs: use CurseForge, Wago, WoWInterface or the author's GitHub (ElvUI only from tukui.org). A real addon is just .lua/.toc/.xml/.tga/.blp files, so delete anything with an .exe, .bat or .dll. Skip "FPS optimizers", automation tools and WeakAuras strings from strangers. Back up your WTF and Interface folders before big changes.

    Systems to use
    • Camping: unlocked by a quest in the starting zone around level 5. Tradeskills upgrade the rest buff from skill 20.
    • Legacy: points are account-wide and spent per character. Unlocks at 15, 25, 40 and 55 points.
    • Hardcore arrives in Winter 2026/27, if you want a second character later.

    Appendix

    Addon setup

    One-time steps to connect the game to this page. Skip this once the From the game card shows your character.

    1. Once Forever is installed, run .\tools\install-addon.ps1 from the GitHub repo (link in Sources). Then enable Grimoire Sync on the character select screen. If it's flagged as out of date, tick "Load out of date AddOns".
    2. Play. Type /grimoire in chat to check what it recorded.
    3. Log out or /reload. WoW writes the file to World of Warcraft\<forever folder>\WTF\Account\<ACCOUNT>\SavedVariables\GrimoireSync.lua
    4. GitHub site: keep .\tools\sync.ps1 -Watch running and the site updates itself after each logout. Claude page: load the file with the button in From the game.

    Blizzard's official character API doesn't support Forever yet. Once it does, this page can read your character directly from your Battle.net account.
